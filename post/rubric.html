Scoring Rubric

Category 4 — Strong and thoughtful 3 — Meets expectations 2 — Developing 1 — Beginning
Inquiry Focused, meaningful question guides the work; team follows promising leads and refines its thinking using what it learns. Clear question guides the work; team completes relevant inquiry and makes some adjustments as needed. Question is broad or unevenly connected to the work; inquiry is limited or inconsistently documented. Question is unclear or missing; little evidence of purposeful inquiry.
Creativity Generates varied possibilities before choosing; prototype or proposal shows original, well-considered thinking. Uses a brainstorm method to develop multiple possibilities; chosen idea is relevant and thoughtfully developed. Generates a limited range of ideas or moves quickly to a first solution; development is uneven. Shows little evidence of brainstorming or idea development.
Evidence use Connects observations and user feedback clearly to the recommendation; represents findings accurately and notes limitations. Student is able to cite reputable sources where relevant. Uses relevant evidence to support the recommendation; findings are mostly represented accurately. Uses some relevant evidence, but links to the recommendation are weak or unclear. Recommendation is mostly unsupported or evidence is misrepresented.
Feasibility Identifies likely users, resources, constraints, and an actionable next step. Idea is practical enough to explore; identifies some resources or next steps. Feasibility is only partly considered; key constraints or steps are missing. Idea lacks a clear path to test or explore.
Communication Pitch is clear, engaging, well organized, student(s) responds thoughtfully to questions. Pitch is illustrated on a slide and submitted in Google Classroom. Pitch explains the need, evidence, and recommendation. Google Classroom submission is present but unclear or not robust. Pitch includes some key points but is difficult to follow or unevenly shared. Pitch is incomplete or does not clearly explain the recommendation. No Google Classroom submission.
